6.1 Standard Fixed-Price Service

   ● Customer defined service select karta hai.

       revised quote zaroori hogi.

       additional kaam start nahi hoga.

   ● Jahan approved policy apply karti ho, already paid Inspection Fee
       same booking ke approved labour/service amount mein adjust ki ja
       sakti hai.

   ● Customer se wohi Inspection Fee dobara charge nahi ki jayegi jo
       already paid ya adjusted ho chuki ho.

   ● Eligible Ustaads Customer ki job par offers bhej sakte hain.
