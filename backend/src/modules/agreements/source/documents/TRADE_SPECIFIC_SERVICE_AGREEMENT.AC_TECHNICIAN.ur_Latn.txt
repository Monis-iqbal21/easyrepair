Agreement se alag document hai.

   ● Har trade ka apna title aur version hoga;
   ● Complete document parhne aur download karne ka option hoga;
   ● Checkbox pehle se selected nahi hoga;
   ● Har acceptance ka alag Unique Acceptance ID hoga;
   ● Accepted document ka complete snapshot aur hash save hoga;
   ● Ustaad ko acceptance PDF download karne ka option diya jayega.

   ● Required assessment separately complete karna hoga;
   ● Trade Agreement separately accept karna hoga.

deti.

COMMON TRADE-SPECIFIC TERMS

Yeh common terms Electrician, Plumber, AC Technician aur Carpenter ke
har Trade-Specific Agreement par apply hongi.

1. GENERAL AGREEMENT KE SAATH TALLUQ

1.1 Yeh Trade-Specific Service Agreement, General Ustaad Service
Provider Agreement ka additional hissa hai.
1.2 General Agreement ki tamam applicable conditions continue rahengi.
1.3 Jahan is Trade Agreement mein safety, scope ya competence ki zyada
specific condition di gayi ho, wahan woh specific condition follow hogi.

2. SIRF APPROVED SERVICES

2.1 Ustaad sirf woh individual service tags perform karega jo uske
HandyGo account par enabled hon.
2.2 Service approval in cheezon par based ho sakti hai:

   ● Documented skills;
   ● Experience;
   ● Training aur assessment;

   ● Required tools;
   ● Testing equipment;
   ● PPE;
   ● Background verification;
   ● Service-specific conditions.

2.3 Account par enable na hone wali service perform nahi ki jayegi.

3. POORE TRADE KI AUTOMATIC PERMISSION NAHI

3.1 Sirf Electrician, Plumber, AC Technician ya Carpenter ke taur par
approval milne ka matlab yeh nahi ke Ustaad us trade ka har installation,
repair, diagnosis ya specialist kaam kar sakta hai.

3.2 Har service ki permission account par enabled service tag ke mutabiq
hogi.

4. TECHNICAL AUR LEGAL REQUIREMENTS

Ustaad applicable situation mein yeh requirements follow karega:

   ● Pakistan aur Sindh ke applicable qawaneen;
   ● Regulator aur utility requirements;
   ● Building aur society rules;
   ● Manufacturer ki instructions;
   ● Equipment ki safe ratings;
   ● Active HandyGo Service Catalog;
   ● Customer-approved booking scope;
   ● HandyGo Trade Academy requirements;
   ● General SOPs;
   ● Incorporated HandyGo policies.

5. COMPETENCE, TOOLS AUR PPE

5.1 Ustaad koi service accept nahi karega jab uske paas required:

   ● Assessed skill;
   ● Safe tools;
   ● Testing equipment;
   ● PPE;
   ● Transport;
   ● Safe access arrangement;
   ● Required helper ya team

available na ho.

5.2 Customer ki urgency unsafe tool, missing PPE ya out-of-skill work ko
allowed nahi banati.

6. STOP-WORK RULE

Ustaad kaam rokega, decline karega ya HandyGo ko escalate karega agar:

   ● Safe isolation confirm na ho;
   ● Job approved service tag se bahar ho;
   ● Required tools ya PPE available na hon;
   ● Customer unsafe ya illegal shortcut kahe;
   ● Unexpected serious hazard samne aaye;
   ● Scope mein important tabdeeli ho;
   ● Kaam safe tareeqe se perform na kiya ja sake.

7. CUSTOMER KI APPROVAL

Customer ki recorded approval aur jahan required ho Operations approval
ke baghair yeh kaam nahi hoga:

   ● Additional trade work;
   ● Extra part ya material;
   ● Additional service;
   ● Opening ya demolition;
   ● Specialist equipment;
   ● Equipment rental;
   ● Revised price;
   ● Different specification;
   ● Scope increase.

8. EVIDENCE AUR TESTING

8.1 Ustaad sirf service area ki minimum necessary photos, videos aur
evidence record karega.

8.2 Ustaad apni approved competence ke andar required final checks
karega.

8.3 Yeh serious violations hain:

   ● Fake test;
   ● Fake meter reading;
   ● Fake calibration;
   ● Fake diagnosis;
   ● Fake photo;
   ● Fake completion record;
   ● Jhooti technical report.

9. HIGHER-RISK SERVICE ACCESS

9.1 Zyada technical ya safety risk wali service tabhi enable hogi jab
Ustaad:

   ● Applicable assessment pass kare;
   ● Specialist tools rakhta ho;
   ● Required PPE rakhta ho;
   ● Required certificate ya credential provide kare;
   ● Service tag ke liye explicit approval hasil kare.
9.2 Higher-risk service approval policy ya incident ke mutabiq dobara
assess ki ja sakti hai.

10. CROSS-TRADE WORK

10.1 Agar job mein kisi doosre trade ka kaam required ho to Ustaad
andazay ya hidden helper ke through woh kaam nahi karega.
10.2 Har required trade skill separately approved honi chahiye.
10.3 Doosre trade ka kaam official HandyGo coordination ke through
karwaya jayega.

SCHEDULE A — AC TECHNICIAN
TRADE-SPECIFIC AGREEMENT

A1. AAM APPROVED SERVICES

Account par enabled service tags ke mutabiq approved work mein yeh
shamil ho sakta hai:

   ● AC cleaning;
   ● General Service;
   ● Master Service;
   ● Accessible filter inspection;
   ● Accessible drain inspection;
   ● Accessible component inspection;
   ● Safe isolation ke baad basic diagnosis;
   ● Approved AC installation;
   ● Approved AC dismounting;
   ● Approved AC relocation.

Installation, dismounting ya relocation tabhi ki jayegi jab:

   ● Access suitable ho;
   ● Mounting suitable ho;
   ● Line-set condition suitable ho;
   ● Electrical condition suitable ho.

A2. ADDITIONAL APPROVAL YA SPECIALIST
COORDINATION

In services ke liye explicit service approval, suitable equipment aur required
specialist coordination zaroori hogi:

   ● Refrigerant leak testing;

   ● Sealed system kholna;
   ● Refrigerant recovery;
   ● Evacuation;
   ● Refrigerant charging;
   ● Brazing;
   ● Hot work;
   ● Compressor replacement;
   ● Major component replacement;
   ● Specialist inverter work;
   ● Specialist PCB work;
   ● Difficult-height access;
   ● Heavy lifting;
   ● Unfamiliar refrigerant;
   ● Flammable refrigerant;
   ● Nayi electrical supply ya circuit modification.

A3. PROHIBITED YA REFER KIYE JANE WALE KAAM

Separate specialist program ke baghair AC Technician yeh kaam nahi
karega:

   ● Chiller work;
   ● VRF ya VRV system;
   ● Central AC plant;
   ● Cold room;
   ● Industrial refrigeration;
   ● Proper controls aur competent team ke baghair unsafe roof work;
   ● Unsafe building facade ya ledge access;
   ● Unknown ya mixed refrigerant transfer;
   ● Refrigerant ka uncontrolled release;
   ● Approved fire aur ventilation controls ke baghair hot work;
   ● Structurally unsafe surface par AC installation;
   ● Inadequate bracket par installation;
   ● Burnt ya flooded electrical supply par work;

   ● Approved electrical skill ke baghair electrical modification.

A4. REFRIGERANT CONTROLS

   ● Connection se pehle refrigerant ki identity aur compatibility confirm ki
       jayegi.

   ● Bina diagnosis ke andhay tareeqe se gas top-up nahi ki jayegi.
   ● Leak investigate kiye baghair bar-bar charging nahi ki jayegi.
   ● Refrigerant directly hawa mein release nahi kiya jayega.
   ● Refrigerant cylinder ka misuse nahi hoga.
   ● Damaged ya unavailable gauges ki surat mein kaam roka jayega.
   ● Damaged hoses ki surat mein kaam roka jayega.
   ● Required leak-detection equipment available na ho to service roki

       jayegi.
   ● Required vacuum ya recovery equipment available na ho to kaam

       roka jayega.
   ● Unsuitable cylinder use nahi kiya jayega.
   ● Refrigerant-related record mein performed process, readings,

       evidence aur limitations sachai se record ki jayengi.

A5. ACCESS, LIFTING AUR PROPERTY CONTROLS

   ● Unstable stool use nahi hoga.
   ● Unsafe solo heavy lifting nahi hogi.
   ● Uncontrolled exterior access nahi hoga.
   ● Indoor AC cleaning ke liye pani aur sludge collect karne ka

       arrangement hoga.
   ● Furniture aur floor ko protect kiya jayega.
   ● Naya electrical circuit ya breaker work approved Electrician

       coordination ke baghair nahi hoga.

A6. QUALITY CLOSURE

Kaam complete karne se pehle:

   ● Panels restore kiye jayen;
   ● Filters restore kiye jayen;
   ● Covers restore kiye jayen;
   ● Drainage check ki jaye;
   ● Work ki wajah se koi leakage na ho;
   ● Mounting secure ho;
   ● Bracket secure ho;
   ● Line routing secure ho;
   ● Scope ke mutabiq functional run observe kiya jaye;
   ● Indoor area clean aur dry kiya jaye;
   ● Customer ko use aur relevant guidance di jaye.
